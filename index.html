      How to play

      The goal

      Survive as long as you can. There is no timer and no win screen: the brainrot keeps spawning faster and in bigger waves until it gets you. Your survival time is your score.

      Everyone plays the same arena each day. It changes at 00:00 UTC, so today's best time is the one to beat. You can retry as often as you like.

      Controls

      WASD
      Move (arrow keys work too)
      MOUSE
      Aim
      CLICK
      Slap, swing or shoot. Hold to keep attacking
      SPACE
      Dash. With a melee weapon you slash everything along the dash
      1
      Kamehameha, once the KI meter is full
      SCROLL
      Zoom the camera
      ESC
      Pause

      On a phone: the left stick moves, SLAP attacks and auto-aims at the nearest enemy, DASH dashes, and KA appears when the Kamehameha is ready.

      Weapons and pickups

      Walk over a glowing pad to grab what is on it. Pads refill after a while.

      • Bare hand never breaks. Most enemies go down in one slap, the tougher ones in two.
      • Wet Fish, Frying Pan, La Chancla, Mega Glove, Samurai Sword hit harder or wider and wear out after a number of hits.
      • Rizz Pistol, Skibidi Shotgun, Gyatt Gatling have limited ammo. You go back to your hands when they run dry.
      • Aura Juice heals 35.
      • Kamehameha: every kill charges KI. At 25, press 1 for 10 seconds of beam. You can't be hurt while it fires, then you're back on your weapon.

      Know your brainrot

      Most go down in one slap and the tough ones take two. The fish, pan, mega glove, samurai sword and the Kamehameha drop anything in one hit. La Chancla and each bullet count as one slap.
